Weekly schedule

Each event enables (NO) or disables (NC) the selected relays at a time on the chosen days. The most recent past event wins; simultaneous events apply together.

Macros

A macro runs a timed series of relay actions. Each step waits its delay (seconds) after the previous step, then applies its action to the selected relays. Steps set relay overrides — use an Auto action to hand a relay back to the schedule. Save before running.
